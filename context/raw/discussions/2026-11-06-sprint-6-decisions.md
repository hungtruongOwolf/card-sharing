# 2026-11-06 — sprint 6 (boards) decisions

Source: EM chat reply "ok" (translated) to six proposed defaults after the sprint-6 report.

The EM accepted all six defaults:

1. A card pinned to a shared board is visible only to members who were sent that card; others see a private tile
   (AC-19.5 resolved in favour of AC-19.7).
2. Board invite/join push notifications follow the existing `friend` toggle; no separate `board` toggle for now.
3. The minor rule on shared boards applies even when the card owner is not a board member (stricter than the spec).
4. The re-invite cooldown covers declined, dropped and expired invitations alike.
5. The board screen uses a Refresh button instead of pull-to-refresh (avoids clashing with one-finger pan).
6. The one-time pin disclosure is remembered per device and cleared on sign-out; no per-account server flag.
