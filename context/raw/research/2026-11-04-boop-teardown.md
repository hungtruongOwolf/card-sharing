# Competitor teardown — Boop: Catch Every Dog & Cat

- iPhone/iPad, solo dev Taiwo Omisore (UK). Shipaton 2026 entry, 1st place #BuildInPublic award. Not yet rated.
- Tagline: "Everyone stops to pet the cute dog."
- Core loop: point camera at a real pet → on-device breed ID (180+ breeds, photos stay on phone,
  says when unsure for mixed breeds) → depth sensing verifies it's a real 3D animal (anti-cheat vs photo of screen)
  → pet becomes a collectible sticker.
- Tactile stickers: squish / bark / meow on touch; holding a cat makes phone purr (haptics).
- Gacha: "Shiny" chance 1/25, 1/100, 1/1000; rarity tiers Common → Legendary; breed facts.
- Breed Book (Pokédex-style), 100 achievements, leaderboard.
- Battle cards with stats from breed data; local friend battles (best-of-3, two phones or pass-and-play).
- Card packs earned (not purchasable), opened by tearing with finger; holo finishes Shiny/Radiant/Celestial/Mythic.
- Send pet to friend, AR display of cards.
- Journal-style sticker book (pages, backgrounds, Book Mode), museum portraits, map pinning where found,
  share/collector cards, name friends' pets. "Pet of the Day" widget.
- Monetization: catching free forever; Boop Pro subscription = unlimited book pages, full map & breed book,
  all backdrops/finishes, AR, book export. 5% of purchases to animal charities.
- App Store: apps.apple.com/us/app/boop-catch-every-dog-cat/id6794737842
