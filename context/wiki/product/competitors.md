# Competitors & References
**TL;DR:** Boop proves "tactile collectible stickers + pets" is engaging; Locket proves
widget-based close-friend sharing. Gap: nobody combines private sharing with a creative scrapbook canvas.

| App | Core | Strengths to learn | Gaps / our wedge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boop (iOS, solo dev) | Catch *strangers'* pets → breed ID → collectible sticker | Squish/sound/haptic stickers, holo finishes, pack-tearing, gacha rarity, sticker book, map, widget, free core + Pro sub | Collection game, not *your* memories; no friend feed; no free-form canvas; iOS only |
| Locket | Photo to friends' home-screen widget | Zero-friction, intimate, viral via invites | No organizing, no creativity, memories vanish into history |

## Boop details worth borrowing
- On-device ML (privacy), depth check against fake captures, "unsure" honesty for mixed breeds.
- Haptics + sound make digital objects feel physical.
- Earned (not bought) packs → retention without pay-to-win.
- Monetize decoration/capacity (pages, backdrops, finishes, export), keep core loop free.
